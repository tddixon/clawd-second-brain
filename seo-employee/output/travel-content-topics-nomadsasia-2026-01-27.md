## Content Production Notes

### Publishing Strategy
1. **Month 1:** Topics 1-10 (high priority, establish local expertise)
2. **Month 2-3:** Topics 11-20 (build content library, target different traveler types)
3. **Ongoing:** Topics 21-25 (fill gaps, seasonal updates, evergreen content)

### Word Counts
- High priority guides: 2,000-2,500 words (comprehensive, rankable)
- Medium priority: 1,500-2,000 words (helpful but focused)
- Lower priority: 1,000-1,500 words (concise, specific)

### Content Angles That Work for Nomads Asia
✅ "Social hostel perspective" (meet people, group activities)  
✅ "Solo traveler friendly" (safety, buddy systems, not being alone)  
✅ "Backpacker budget" (real prices, money-saving tips)  
✅ "Local knowledge" (staff recommendations, hidden spots)  
✅ "Honest & practical" (no BS, real expectations, what sucks vs what's great)  

### Natural Hostel Integration
Every article should include:
- Map showing proximity to your hostel location(s)
- "Our guests recommend..." or "Ask reception for..."
- Social angle: "Find travel buddies at the hostel for this activity"
- Budget tip that naturally mentions affordable hostel accommodation
- Booking CTA at end: "Book your stay in Ao Nang" (not pushy, just convenient)

### SEO Integration
- Primary keyword in title, H1, first paragraph, naturally throughout
- Long-tail variations in H2 subheadings
- Internal linking: Each article links to 3-5 other related articles
- Location pages: Link to your Krabi/Ao Nang hostel pages when relevant
- Social sharing: Optimize for Pinterest (visual guides) and Reddit (helpful, not spammy)

### Success Metrics to Track
- Time on page (target: 3+ minutes)
- Scroll depth (target: 70%+)
- Social shares (Pinterest saves, Reddit upvotes)
- Comments ("This helped!" = success)
- Conversion to booking page visits
- Backlinks from travel forums
- Search rankings (secondary to engagement)
