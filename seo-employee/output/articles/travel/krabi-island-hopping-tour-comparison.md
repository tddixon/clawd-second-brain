# Krabi Island Hopping: 4 Islands Tour vs Hong Islands vs DIY (Which One?)

Island hopping is THE activity in Krabi. Everyone does it, and honestly, you should too - the islands around Krabi are stunning.

But here's where people get stuck: There are multiple tour options, they all sound similar, and you're sitting at your hostel trying to figure out if the "4 Islands Tour" is better than the "Hong Islands Tour" and whether you should just rent a private longtail and go rogue.

I'm going to break down each option with real prices, actual itineraries, and honest opinions about which tour is right for different types of travelers. By the end, you'll know exactly which one to book.

## The Quick Comparison

| Tour | Price | Duration | Crowds | Best For |
|------|-------|----------|--------|----------|
| **4 Islands Tour** | 700-900฿ | 7-8hrs | High | First-timers, budget, social |
| **Hong Islands Tour** | 1,200-1,500฿ | 7-8hrs | Medium | Better snorkeling, scenery |
| **7 Islands Tour** | 1,000-1,300฿ | 9-10hrs | High | Maximizers (exhausting) |
| **DIY Private Longtail** | 2,500-4,000฿ | Flexible | Just you | Groups of 6+, custom plans |

**TL;DR Answer:**

- **First time in Krabi?** 4 Islands Tour
- **Want the best scenery/snorkeling?** Hong Islands Tour
- **Traveling solo and want to meet people?** 4 Islands Tour (easiest to find boat buddies)
- **Group of 6+ friends?** Split a private longtail, go DIY
- **Trying to "see everything" in one day?** Don't. Pick one tour and enjoy it.

## 4 Islands Tour (The Most Popular One)

**Price:** 700-900 baht ($20-25) | **Duration:** 7-8 hours | **Departs:** 8:30-9am, returns 4-5pm

This is the tour 70% of Ao Nang visitors book. It's popular for a reason - good value, hits the main spots, easy to organize, and you'll meet other travelers.

### What's Included:
- Hotel pickup from Ao Nang
- Longtail boat + driver
- Life jackets
- Snorkel gear (mask + fins - quality varies)
- Lunch (usually boxed lunch on the boat, sometimes buffet on an island)
- Water and soft drinks
- National park fees
- Guide (often speaks minimal English, but you don't really need much guidance)

### The Actual Itinerary (What You'll Actually Do):

**Stop 1: Phra Nang Cave Beach (Railay)** - 45-60 minutes
- The famous Instagram beach with limestone cliffs
- Perfect white sand, turquoise water
- Explore the cave with the wooden phallus shrine (yes, really)
- Swim, take photos, try not to laugh at the fertility shrine
- **Reality check:** You're here with 10 other tour boats. It's crowded but still beautiful.

**Stop 2: Tup Island & Chicken Island** - 1-1.5 hours
- **Tup Island:** Connected to Mor Island by a sandbar that appears at low tide (you can walk between islands)
- **Chicken Island:** Named for a rock formation that looks like a chicken head (you'll see it)
- Good snorkeling here - clear water, lots of fish, some coral
- Swimming and beach time
- **Reality check:** The sandbar is cool if the timing is right (check tide times). Snorkeling here is decent, not world-class.

**Stop 3: Poda Island** - 1-1.5 hours
- Lunch stop (most tours do lunch here)
- Beautiful beach, good swimming
- Less crowded than Phra Nang
- Lots of photo ops with the limestone formations
- **Reality check:** The beach is nice, but the main highlight is the food break and relaxing.

**Stop 4: Si Island (Koh Si)** - 30-45 minutes
- Snorkeling stop (sometimes skipped if running late)
- Small island, clear water
- Best snorkeling spot of the four islands
- You're in the water most of this stop
- **Reality check:** Snorkeling here is good for tropical fish, okay coral. Don't expect Maldives-level visibility.

### The Good:
- Covers the main highlights (Phra Nang Cave Beach is worth it alone)
- Cheap
- Social - you'll be with other travelers (good for solo travelers)
- Easy to book (every hostel and travel agency sells this)
- Includes everything - you just show up

### The Bad:
- Crowded - you're sharing these islands with dozens of other tour boats
- Rushed schedule - 45-60 mins per stop isn't much
- Cookie-cutter experience - every tour does the exact same thing
- Snorkel gear quality varies (sometimes foggy masks)
- Lunch is usually basic (think fried rice in a box, maybe some fruit)

### Who Should Book This:
- First-timers who want to hit the main spots
- Solo travelers (easy to meet people on group tours)
- Budget-conscious travelers
- Anyone who wants a guaranteed good day without planning

### Who Should Skip This:
- People who've been to tropical islands before and want something less touristy
- Photographers who want empty beach shots (these islands are packed)
- Serious snorkelers (Hong Islands is better for snorkeling)

**Book through:** Your hostel (they'll get you the same tour as booking.com but cheaper, and help you coordinate with other guests)

**Pro tip:** If you're solo, find 2-3 other solo travelers at your hostel going the same day. Instant friends, and you'll hang together all day.

## Hong Islands Tour (The Better Scenery Option)

**Price:** 1,200-1,500 baht ($33-42) | **Duration:** 7-8 hours | **Departs:** 8:30-9am, returns 4-5pm

This tour costs more but goes to less-crowded islands with better snorkeling and more dramatic scenery.

### What's Included:
Same as 4 Islands Tour (pickup, boat, gear, lunch, park fees)

### The Actual Itinerary:

**Stop 1: Hong Lagoon (Emerald Lagoon)** - 30-45 minutes
- Paddle into a hidden lagoon surrounded by limestone cliffs
- Transfer to canoes/kayaks to enter (longtails can't fit)
- Incredibly scenic - feels like Jurassic Park
- Calm, green water
- Great photos
- **Reality check:** This stop alone makes the tour worth it. Stunning and unique.

**Stop 2: Lading Island (Koh Lading)** - 1 hour
- Small island, beautiful beach
- Good snorkeling off the beach
- Clearer water than 4 Islands spots
- More fish and healthier coral
- **Reality check:** This is what people imagine when they think "tropical island snorkeling."

**Stop 3: Pakbia Island** - 1-1.5 hours
- Lunch stop
- Beach time, swimming, relaxing
- Less developed than Poda Island (fewer tour groups)
- Nice white sand beach

**Stop 4: Hong Island Beach** - 1-1.5 hours
- Main island, bigger beach
- Swimming and beach time
- More space to spread out than 4 Islands beaches
- Beautiful scenery (limestone cliffs everywhere)

**Stop 5: Daeng & Rai Islands (snorkeling)** - 30-45 minutes
- Final snorkeling stop
- Best coral of the tour
- Clear water, lots of fish
- You're in the water, not on a beach

### The Good:
- Better snorkeling (clearer water, healthier coral, more fish)
- Less crowded (fewer tour boats go here)
- The Hong Lagoon is incredible and unique
- More scenic overall (the limestone formations are more dramatic)
- Lunch is usually better (some tours include restaurant buffet)

### The Bad:
- More expensive (50% more than 4 Islands)
- Slightly less social (fewer boats = fewer travelers to meet)
- Longer boat ride to get there (the islands are further from Ao Nang)
- Same rushed schedule (still only 45-60 mins per stop)

### Who Should Book This:
- Anyone who can afford the extra $15-20
- Snorkeling enthusiasts
- Photographers (better scenery, fewer crowds)
- People who've done the standard island tours before
- Anyone prioritizing quality over budget

### Who Should Skip This:
- Strict budget travelers (4 Islands is fine and cheaper)
- People prone to seasickness (longer boat ride)

**Book through:** Your hostel or reputable tour agencies (avoid random beach vendors - they mark up significantly)

**Pro tip:** If you're debating between 4 Islands and Hong Islands, and you can afford the extra 400-500 baht, go Hong Islands. The experience is notably better.

## 7 Islands Tour (The "See Everything" Option)

**Price:** 1,000-1,300 baht ($28-36) | **Duration:** 9-10 hours (long day) | **Departs:** 8am, returns 5-6pm

This tour tries to cram in way more stops. It sounds impressive ("seven islands!") but it's exhausting and you spend more time on the boat than enjoying islands.

### What's Included:
Same as above (pickup, boat, gear, lunch, park fees)

### The Itinerary:
Basically combines elements of the 4 Islands and Hong Islands tours, plus a few extra quick stops. You'll hit:
- Phra Nang Cave Beach
- Chicken Island
- Tup Island
- Poda Island
- Hong Lagoon (sometimes)
- A couple other islands for quick snorkeling

### The Reality:
- 9-10 hours is a LONG day on a longtail boat
- You're spending 20-30 minutes per island (barely enough time to get off the boat, swim, get back on)
- It's rushed and exhausting
- You don't get to really enjoy any single stop
- By island 5, you're tired and over it
- The boat ride back feels endless

### Who Should Book This:
- People with limited time who want to "see everything" (not recommended, but I get it)
- Maximizers who hate feeling like they missed something

### Who Should Skip This:
- Most people. Seriously, pick either 4 Islands OR Hong Islands and actually enjoy it. Seven islands sounds great on paper but it's just stressful.

**Honest recommendation:** Skip this one. It's trying too hard.

## DIY Private Longtail Boat (The Flexible Option)

**Price:** 2,500-4,000 baht for the whole boat (split among your group) | **Duration:** Flexible (you decide) | **Departs:** Whenever you want

If you have 6-8 people, you can hire a private longtail boat and create your own itinerary.

### How It Works:
- Negotiate with longtail boat drivers at Ao Nang Beach
- Tell them which islands you want to visit
- Agree on price, duration, and departure time
- They'll suggest routes and timing

### The Good:
- Total flexibility (stay longer at beaches you love, skip ones you don't)
- No waiting for other tour groups
- You pick departure and return times
- Can go to less-visited spots
- Great for groups of friends who want to do their own thing

### The Bad:
- More expensive (only worth it if you split among 6-8 people)
- You have to plan the route (the driver will help, but you need ideas)
- No lunch included (bring your own or stop at a restaurant island)
- No snorkel gear included (rent separately, 100-150 baht per person)
- Language barrier (many longtail drivers speak limited English)

### The Math:
- Private boat: 3,000 baht
- Split among 6 people: 500 baht per person (similar to 4 Islands Tour price)
- But add gear rental, lunch, drinks: +300-400 baht per person
- **Total: 800-900 baht per person**

So it's only cheaper than Hong Islands Tour if you have a full group and bring your own food.

### Who Should Do This:
- Groups of 6+ friends traveling together
- People who've been before and know which islands they want to revisit
- Photographers who want to arrive early/late to avoid crowds
- Anyone who hates being on a schedule

### Who Should Skip This:
- Solo travelers or couples (way too expensive)
- First-timers who don't know the area
- Anyone who doesn't want the planning hassle

**How to book:** Walk to Ao Nang Beach, talk to longtail boat drivers. Negotiate. Get a phone number, confirm the day before. Cash only.

**Pro tip:** Go the day before, scout the drivers, get quotes from 3-4 different drivers. Pick one, get their phone number, confirm in the morning of your trip.

## Speedboat vs Longtail Boat Tours

Some operators offer speedboat versions of these tours (usually double the price). Here's the deal:

**Speedboat pros:**
- Faster (you get to islands quicker, more time there)
- More comfortable
- Better for seasickness (smoother ride)
- Sometimes includes nicer lunch

**Speedboat cons:**
- Expensive (1,800-2,500 baht)
- Less "authentic" Thai experience
- You're still with a tour group (same crowding issues)

**Should you pay extra for speedboat?**

Maybe, if:
- You get seasick easily
- You value comfort
- You want 30-45 more minutes at each island
- You can afford the extra $20-30

Skip if:
- You're on a budget (longtail is fine)
- You like the traditional Thai longtail experience

**Honest take:** Longtail boats are part of the experience. Save the money unless you have a good reason (seasickness, back problems, etc.).

## Booking Tips

**Where to book:**
- **Your hostel** (best option - they know reliable operators, help coordinate with other guests, get you fair prices)
- **Local tour agencies in Ao Nang** (walk-in prices are often negotiable)
- **Online platforms** (convenient but usually 10-20% markup)

**When to book:**
- 1-2 days ahead is fine
- High season (Dec-Feb): Book 2-3 days ahead
- Last-minute same-day bookings possible but tours might be full

**What's included (double-check):**
- Hotel pickup
- National park fees (300 baht - should be included)
- Snorkel gear
- Lunch
- Water/drinks
- Life jackets

**Scam warning:**
Some beach vendors quote super low prices (500 baht for 4 Islands!) then add fees later (park fees, gear rental, "fuel surcharge"). Book through your hostel or established agencies.

**Group bookings:**
If 4+ people from your hostel are going, ask for a group discount (50-100 baht off per person is normal).

## What to Bring

**Essential:**
- Sunscreen (reef-safe if possible - protect the coral)
- Waterproof bag or case for phone
- Swimsuit (wear it under clothes)
- Towel (some tours provide, some don't)
- Water bottle (refill from tour's water supply)
- Cash (for tips, extra snacks)

**Recommended:**
- Underwater camera or GoPro (iPhone in a waterproof case works too)
- Seasickness pills if you're prone (take 30 mins before departure)
- Light long-sleeve shirt (sun protection on the boat)
- Hat and sunglasses
- Flip-flops or water shoes

**Don't bring:**
- Valuables (leave in your hostel locker)
- Too much stuff (you're getting on and off boats all day)
- Expectations of empty beaches (these are popular tours)

## Real Talk: What to Actually Expect

**The water:** Gorgeous. Turquoise, clear, warm. You'll want to swim at every stop.

**The snorkeling:** Good, not amazing. You'll see tropical fish and some coral. It's not Great Barrier Reef level, but it's fun and beautiful.

**The crowds:** Significant on 4 Islands Tour, moderate on Hong Islands Tour. Phra Nang Cave Beach is always packed. Accept it and enjoy anyway.

**The boat ride:** Bumpy if the sea is choppy. You'll get splashed. It's part of the experience.

**The lunch:** Functional, not gourmet. Thai fried rice, maybe some chicken or fish, fruit. It fills you up.

**The schedule:** Tight. You're on a schedule and the guide will rush you back to the boat.

**The other tourists:** Mix of backpackers, couples, families, Asian tour groups. Everyone's friendly. Solo travelers easily meet people.

**Weather:** Tours run in rainy season but might get cancelled if seas are too rough. In dry season (Nov-Apr), tours run 95% of the time.

## Which Tour Should YOU Book?

**If this is your first island hopping experience:** 4 Islands Tour. It's the classic for a reason.

**If you've done island tours before and want quality:** Hong Islands Tour. Worth the extra money.

**If you're traveling solo:** 4 Islands Tour (easier to meet people).

**If you're with a big group:** DIY private longtail.

**If you're short on time:** Skip the 7 Islands Tour. Do one tour well instead of seven islands rushed.

**If you want the absolute best Krabi has to offer:** Hong Islands Tour + separate Railay day trip.

**If you're on a tight budget:** 4 Islands Tour. It's solid value.

## FAQ: Krabi Island Tours

**Q: Can I do both 4 Islands and Hong Islands tours?**
You can, but it's repetitive. Pick one. If you have extra days, do Railay separately or a different activity.

**Q: What if the weather is bad?**
Tours get cancelled if seas are too rough (rare, but happens in rainy season). You'll get a refund or reschedule.

**Q: Can I swim at every stop?**
Yes, that's the point. Bring your swimsuit and get in the water.

**Q: Is snorkeling safe for beginners?**
Yes. Life jackets provided, guides keep an eye on everyone, water is shallow near islands.

**Q: Do I need to book in advance?**
1-2 days ahead is smart, especially in high season. Last-minute is possible but tours might be full.

**Q: Are there bathrooms on the islands?**
Some islands have basic toilets, others don't. Go before you leave, and the boat will stop somewhere with facilities during the day.

## Bottom Line

You can't go wrong with either the 4 Islands or Hong Islands tour. Pick based on your budget and priorities:
- **Budget + social:** 4 Islands Tour
- **Better scenery + snorkeling:** Hong Islands Tour

Book through your hostel in Ao Nang - they'll help coordinate with other guests, get you fair prices, and answer questions.

This is one of the highlights of Krabi. Even with crowds, even with the rushed schedule, the islands are stunning. Just go.
