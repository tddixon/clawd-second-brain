# Ao Nang Walking Street Night Market: Your Weekend Dinner Plans

The Ao Nang Walking Street Night Market sets up every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday evening, and it's honestly one of the best parts of being in Ao Nang on the weekend.

Cheap food, local crafts, live music, and a chill vibe where tourists and Thai locals mix. It's not massive like Bangkok markets, but it's perfect for dinner and wandering.

Here's everything you need to know to make the most of it.

## When & Where

**Days:** Friday, Saturday, Sunday only  
**Time:** ~5:00pm-10:00pm (vendors start setting up around 4:30pm, wind down around 10pm)  
**Location:** Main street in Ao Nang (the road literally closes for the market)

**How to find it:** Walk toward the main Ao Nang intersection. You'll see the road blocked off and stalls everywhere. Impossible to miss.

**Duration:** Plan 1-2 hours to eat, browse, and soak it in.

## What to Eat (The Main Event)

**Budget:** 150-250 baht ($4-7) for dinner with drinks

### Street Food Stalls (50-100 baht per item)

**Pad Thai (50-70 baht):**
Multiple vendors making it fresh. All taste pretty similar. Just pick whichever has the shortest line.

**Grilled meat skewers (40-80 baht):**
- Pork: 10-15 baht per stick
- Chicken: 10-15 baht per stick
- Squid: 60-80 baht for a whole grilled squid
- Get 4-5 sticks and call it dinner

**Som Tam (papaya salad) - 50 baht:**
Spicy and fresh. If you can't handle heat, say "mai pet" (not spicy). They'll still make it spicy, but less deadly.

**Fried chicken (50-80 baht):**
Thai-style fried chicken with sticky rice. Crispy, garlicky, delicious.

**Roti (30-60 baht):**
- Banana roti: 30-40 baht (dessert)
- Egg roti: 40 baht (savory)
- Nutella roti: 50-60 baht (sugar bomb)

**BBQ seafood (100-200 baht):**
Grilled fish, prawns, etc. More expensive but fresh.

**Fresh fruit smoothies (60-80 baht):**
Mango, pineapple, watermelon. Perfect for the heat.

### Local Restaurants (Open-Air Seating)

Some stalls have seating areas where you order multiple dishes family-style.

**Tom Yum soup:** 80-120 baht  
**Curries with rice:** 100-120 baht  
**Stir-fries:** 80-100 baht

**Pro tip:** Order 2-3 dishes, share with your crew, try more variety.

## What to Buy (Souvenirs & Stuff)

**Clothes:**
- Tank tops, harem pants, elephant print clothes
- Prices: 100-300 baht
- Haggle a bit (they expect it)

**Jewelry:**
- Handmade bracelets, necklaces
- 50-150 baht
- Decent quality for the price

**Art & Crafts:**
- Local paintings (hit or miss)
- Wood carvings
- Handmade bags
- 200-500 baht for art

**Touristy Stuff:**
- Fridge magnets: 30-50 baht
- Keychains: 20-40 baht
- Postcards: 10-20 baht

**Honestly:** The food is the main reason to go. Shopping is just a bonus.

## The Vibe

**Chill and social:**
- Families, backpackers, locals all mixing
- Live music sometimes (Thai bands playing covers)
- String lights everywhere (cute for photos)
- Walking around with food in hand is the move

**Not too crowded:**
It's busy but not shoulder-to-shoulder Bangkok market chaos. You can breathe.

**Good for dates/groups:**
It's a social activity without being a "plan." Just wander, eat, vibe.

## Tips for First-Timers

**Bring cash:**
Most vendors don't take cards. Have 500-1,000 baht in small bills.

**Come hungry:**
You'll want to try multiple things. Don't eat a big lunch.

**Share food:**
Go with friends, order different dishes, share. More variety.

**Haggle (gently):**
For souvenirs, offer 20-30% less than asking price. They'll negotiate. For food, prices are fixed.

**Grab napkins:**
Food stalls have them, take a stack. You'll need them.

**Pace yourself:**
The market is only ~300 meters. Walk slow, stop at stalls, don't rush.

**Take photos:**
The string lights and food stalls are Instagram-worthy at golden hour (6-7pm).

## What It's NOT

**It's NOT:**
- A huge Bangkok-style market (this is small and local)
- Full of unique souvenirs (most stuff is standard tourist items)
- Open every night (only Fri-Sun)
- A replacement for Krabi Town weekend market (that one's bigger and more authentic)

**It IS:**
- A fun weekend evening activity
- Great for cheap dinner
- Social and chill
- Convenient (right in Ao Nang)

## Compared to Other Markets

**Ao Nang Night Market vs Krabi Town Weekend Market:**

**Ao Nang:** Smaller, more tourists, convenient, decent  
**Krabi Town (Saturday):** Way bigger, mostly locals, more authentic, requires transport

**If you're in Ao Nang on Saturday:** Consider going to Krabi Town market instead (or both if you're ambitious).

**If you're here Fri/Sun:** Ao Nang Night Market is your option.

## Sample Night Market Plan

**6:00pm:** Arrive as market is getting busy  
**6:00pm-6:30pm:** Walk through once to scout options  
**6:30pm-7:30pm:** Eat dinner (pad thai + skewers + smoothie)  
**7:30pm-8:30pm:** Browse souvenirs, maybe buy something  
**8:30pm-9:00pm:** Second dessert (roti or ice cream)  
**9:00pm:** Head to beach bars or back to hostel

**Total spent:** 200-300 baht ($5-8)

## Going Solo vs With a Group

**Solo:**
- Totally fine
- Easy to navigate
- Order smaller portions or try street snacks
- Good people-watching

**With hostel crew:**
- Way more fun
- Share dishes, try more variety
- Social atmosphere
- Group photos with food

**Either way works.** This isn't a "need a group" activity.

## What to Skip

**Overpriced tourist traps:**
- Restaurants on the edge of the market charging 300 baht for pad thai (just eat at the stalls)
- "Authentic Thai massage" vendors (get massage at a spa, not the market)
- Knockoff sunglasses that'll break in 2 days

**Focus on food and vibes, skip the rest.**

## After the Market

**9:00pm-10:00pm options:**

**Beach bars:**
5-minute walk to beach, continue the night with drinks

**Hostel hangout:**
Go back, chill in common area with your crew

**7-Eleven run:**
Grab water and snacks for tomorrow

**Early night:**
You've been in the sun all day, sleep is valid

## FAQ

**Q: Is it every night?**
No. Friday, Saturday, Sunday only.

**Q: What if it rains?**
Vendors usually still set up unless it's pouring. Bring an umbrella or wait it out at a covered stall.

**Q: Can I pay with card?**
Mostly cash only. ATMs nearby if needed.

**Q: Is the food safe?**
Yes. High turnover = fresh food. Standard street food safety applies.

**Q: How late does it go?**
Officially 10pm, but vendors start packing up around 9:30pm.
