## Content Gaps

Things competitors aren't covering well that you could own:

1. **Long-term stay guides** - Most content focused on 1-3 night stays, not month-long digital nomad stays
2. **Neighborhood comparisons** - "Khao San vs Sukhumvit vs Silom: Which Bangkok Area for Your Hostel Stay?"
3. **Specific traveler profiles** - Content for solo female, digital nomads, party seekers kept separate
4. **Practical transport guides** - How to get from airport to specific hostels by hostel location
5. **Seasonal considerations** - Rainy season hostel recommendations (pool vs no pool relevance shifts)

## Avoid These (Too Competitive Right Now)

Don't waste time on these until you've built more authority:

- **"best hostels bangkok"** - Dominated by Hostelworld, Nomadic Matt, major travel blogs
- **"bangkok hostels"** - Too broad, booking platforms own this
- **"bangkok accommodation"** - Extremely competitive, hotels outrank hostels

Work toward these in 6-12 months after building topical authority.

**Why This Works:**
- Starts with winnable keywords (build momentum + traffic)
