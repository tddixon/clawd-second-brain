**Change to:** "Social Hostels in Thailand | Nomads Asia - Krabi & More"

**How to fix:**

- Keep "Book Direct & Save 25%" as an H2 or promotional banner text

### Day 7: Google Search Console Setup

**Step 1: Verify your website**
1. Go to search.google.com/search-console
2. Add property: www.nomadsasia.com
3. Verify ownership (HTML tag method is easiest)

**Step 2: Submit sitemap**
1. Generate XML sitemap (most CMS do this automatically at `/sitemap.xml`)
2. In Search Console, go to "Sitemaps"
3. Submit sitemap URL
4. Check back in 2-3 days to see indexing status

**Step 3: Request indexing for new pages**
1. In Search Console, use "URL Inspection" tool
2. Enter each blog article URL
3. Click "Request Indexing"
4. Do this for all 3 articles + any new location pages

**Step 4: Monitor**
- Check "Coverage" report weekly to see what's indexed
- Check "Performance" report to see impressions/clicks as they start coming in

## Risks & Mitigation

### Risk #1: Google doesn't index or rank content

**Likelihood:** Low (if following best practices)  
**Mitigation:**
- Submit sitemap immediately
- Request indexing via Search Console
- Build backlinks to help Google discover pages
- Ensure technical SEO is solid (fast site, mobile-friendly)

**If it happens:** Audit technical issues, check robots.txt, verify no crawl blocks

---

### Risk #2: Keywords are more competitive than estimated

**Likelihood:** Medium  
**Mitigation:**
- Start with quick wins (low competition keywords)
- Build authority before tackling competitive terms
- Focus on long-tail keywords first

**If it happens:** Pivot to even more niche keywords, focus on local/specific terms

---

### Risk #3: Content doesn't convert to bookings

**Likelihood:** Low-Medium  
**Mitigation:**
- Include clear booking CTAs in every article
- Link to location pages prominently
- Track conversions in Google Analytics to optimize

**If it happens:** A/B test different CTAs, improve location pages, add booking incentives

---

### Risk #4: Can't maintain publishing schedule

**Likelihood:** Medium (running a hostel is busy)  
**Mitigation:**
- Batch-create content (write 2-3 articles in one day)
- Hire freelance writer after Month 1
- Reduce to 1 article/week instead of 2 if needed

**If it happens:** Quality > quantity. 1 great article/month beats 4 rushed ones.
