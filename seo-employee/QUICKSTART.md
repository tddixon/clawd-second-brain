## The System

5 skills that work together:

1. **keyword-research** - Find what to target
2. **seo-analyze** - Audit your site or competitors
3. **content-strategy** - Plan what to write
4. **write-article** - Create content
5. **full-pipeline** - Do all of the above automatically

## Example: Complete Workflow

### Day 1: Research
```
Full pipeline for https://competitor.com
```
Review the output, understand their strategy.

### Week 1-4: Execute
From the content strategy you got, write 4 more articles:
```
Write article about: [topic from strategy]
Write article about: [topic from strategy]
Write article about: [topic from strategy]
Write article about: [topic from strategy]
```

**Result:** 7 articles ready to publish (3 from pipeline + 4 additional)

### Month 2-3: Expand
Follow the 3-month calendar from the content strategy.

Use the write-article skill for each article on the calendar.

---

## Tips

1. **Start with full-pipeline** to see everything it can do
2. **Use competitor URLs** to learn from what's working
3. **Follow the content calendar** - it's prioritized for you
4. **Publish consistently** - 1 article/week minimum
5. **All articles are humanized** - they don't sound like AI
