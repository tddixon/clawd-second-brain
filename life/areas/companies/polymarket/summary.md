# Polymarket

**Type:** companies
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-01

## Current Context

### Market

- Polymarket is a decentralized prediction market platform built on Polygon. Uses hybrid-decentralized Central Limit Order Book (CLOB) with off-chain matching and on-chain settlement. Operates binary markets with YES/NO tokens summing to $1.00. Zero trading fees for most markets, UMA oracle for resolution. (01/31/2026)
- Polymarket provides official Python SDK (py-clob-client) for CLOB API integration, plus REST APIs for market data, trades, and order book snapshots. WebSocket feeds available for real-time price updates. EIP-712 signed messages for on-chain settlement. (01/31/2026)
- Top performers: @Frosenn (political markets), French Whale Théo ($85M profit Trump 2024), @Tutaaa91 (arbitrage $58k from $0.02). Suspected institutional firms: Jane Street, Citadel, DRW/Cumberland for market making. (01/31/2026)
- Live opportunity: Fed Chair market showing pricing error at 99¢ total (YES+NO < $1.00), representing 1% risk-free arbitrage. Resolution expected within days. Documented in RESEARCH-OPPORTUNITIES-NOW.md. (01/31/2026)
- High-probability setup: Buy NO at 80¢ (market mispriced - should be 95¢+). Constitutional barriers make Greenland acquisition unlikely. 25% expected return identified in RESEARCH-OPPORTUNITIES-NOW.md. (01/31/2026)

### General

- Primary alpha source: Combinatorial arbitrage (risk-free when YES+NO < $1.00). 50-200 daily opportunities with 0.5-2% profit margins. CLOB model enables tighter spreads and higher liquidity vs traditional AMMs. (01/31/2026)
